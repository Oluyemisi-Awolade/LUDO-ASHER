Privacy Policy — Ludo Asher

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Ludo Asher ("the App", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and how it is protected. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information (via Firebase Authentication):

  • Email address
  • Display name
  • Date of birth, collected when you create an account and used only to decide which chat features are available to you (see Section 2)

Gameplay Data:

  • Win/loss record
  • ELO rating
  • In-game coin balance
  • Games played

Online Multiplayer and Safety Data:

  • Room codes you create or join
  • In-game chat messages sent during online matches
  • Your chosen player color/token position and turn data, shared with other players in the same room in real time
  • Your list of blocked users
  • Reports you submit about other users, and reports submitted about you, and whether an account has been suspended

Purchases: The App offers optional "Support the Developer" in-app purchases through Google Play. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card or other financial details. We receive only confirmation from Google Play that a purchase completed, including the item and quantity purchased.

Analytics: The App does not currently use any analytics or advertising SDKs. If this changes in the future, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect, and the change will be reflected in the app's Data Safety disclosure on Google Play.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your player account
  • To save and display your gameplay statistics (wins, losses, ELO, coins)
  • To enable real-time online multiplayer matches, including syncing game state and chat between players in the same room
  • To display leaderboard and tournament standings
  • To keep chat age-appropriate: your date of birth determines whether you can send free-text messages (18 and over) or preset phrases only
  • To moderate the community, including reviewing reports and suspending accounts that break our Terms of Service
  • To confirm that requests to our servers come from the genuine App (Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity)

3. Third-Party Services

Firebase (Google): Used for authentication, real-time database storage of account and gameplay data, and App Check. Firebase's own privacy practices are described at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Google Play: Used to process optional in-app purchases. Google's handling of your payment information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in Firebase Realtime Database and Firebase Authentication, both operated by Google. We apply access-control rules to this database to restrict who can read or write data. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Your account and gameplay data are retained for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time using the "Delete My Account" option in the App's menu, which removes your account and gameplay data. You can also request deletion by contacting us using the details below, or see our account deletion page. Reports filed for safety review may be retained for a limited period after deletion.

6. Children's Privacy

Ludo Asher is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
