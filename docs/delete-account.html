Ludo Asher — Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: October 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Ludo Asher account and the data associated with it. This applies to the app Ludo Asher (package com.ludopromax.app), developed by Yemisi.

Delete inside the app

Log in, open the menu and tap Delete My Account. Confirm with your password and your account and gameplay data are deleted.

Or request deletion by email

  1. Send an email to oluyemisiitunuolu111@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Ludo Asher account.
  2. Use the subject line: Ludo Asher — Account Deletion Request
  3. Include your account email address in the body of the message so your account can be located.
Request Account Deletion

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email address, display name, date of birth)
  • Gameplay statistics (win/loss record, ELO rating, coin balance, games played)
  • Your blocked-users list
  • Your chat messages sent during online multiplayer matches

What may be retained

  • Reports you filed against other users, or reports filed against you, may be retained for a limited period for moderation and safety review purposes, even after account deletion.
  • Records of in-app purchases are held by Google Play under its own policies, not by us.

Processing time

Deleting inside the app takes effect immediately. Email requests are processed manually and are typically completed within 30 days of a verified request.

Contact

Questions about this process or your data can be sent to oluyemisiitunuolu111@gmail.com.
